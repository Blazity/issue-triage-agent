# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and respectful community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical concerns directly.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and report security issues privately.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through a private GitHub channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
